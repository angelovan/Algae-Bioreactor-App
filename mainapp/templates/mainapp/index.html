Welcome to the Algae Bioreactor STEM Lab!

This application helps students and teachers monitor algae bioreactor units for three different types of algae: Chlorella vulgaris, Arthrospira platensis (Spirulina), and Scenedesmus obliquus.

Additional features include growth progress observation and tracking of feeding schedules.
